# Product Owner — Field References (Purpose & scope)

Living reference notes for the Product Owner's field. From an **independent field audit** (2026-07-02, real subagent). Through-line: define success as outcomes, connect the WHY to a living discovery map, and prioritize by unmet need. Re-verify before citing.

## Critical
- **[Opportunity Solution Trees & Continuous Discovery](https://www.producttalk.org/opportunity-solution-trees/)** — The default structure connecting outcome → customer opportunities → solutions → assumption tests; one living map anchoring all four sub-domains.
- **[Outcomes over Outputs / Build Trap](https://melissaperri.com/book)** — Defining success as customer/business outcomes, not shipped features — the core WHY discipline; its absence is the most common strategy failure.

## Important
- **[North Star ↔ OKR linkage](https://amplitude.com/north-star)** — Distinguish a persistent North Star (strategic value) from point-in-time OKRs, and where strategy sits between them ([strategy-metrics sandwich](https://herbig.co/product-strategy-metrics-sandwich/)).
- **[Assumption Mapping & Riskiest-Assumption Testing](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/how-assumptions-mapping-can-focus-your-teams-on-running-experiments-that-matter)** — Force desirability/viability/feasibility hypotheses onto an importance-vs-evidence grid so the team tests only what could sink the idea.

## Emerging
- **[Prioritization — RICE + ODI](https://www.intercom.com/blog/rice-simple-prioritization-for-product-managers/)** — A fast effort-vs-value model (RICE) plus a demand-side model ([Outcome-Driven Innovation](https://strategyn.com/outcome-driven-innovation-process/)) so priority is grounded in unmet need.
- **[Jobs-to-be-Done for framing the WHY](https://hbr.org/2016/09/know-your-customers-jobs-to-be-done)** — A rigorous way to define the progress a customer seeks before any solution — the input that feeds the opportunity tree.
