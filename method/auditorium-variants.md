# The Auditorium variants — which one to call, by the size of the question

Written 2026-11-15 because a fresh reader, asked how the auditoriums fit together as a progression of
scope, had to assemble the answer from three pages and said the library never states it. **The
semantics of each variant live where they were ruled, in `protocols/triggers.md`; this page copies none
of them.** It only lays them side by side and says which to call. **The ordering by scope is the
Library's reading of those pages. The Composer has not ruled a ranking of the variants by scope.**

The topics declared today are presentation, code, strategy, deployment stability and library, in
`chairs/the-twelve-chairs.md`. A topic outside the table is proposed back to him as buttons and added
once he confirms it.

## Reading them as a progression of scope

One reading, in the order the scope widens:

1. **A question** (the plain Auditorium): breadth over depth, twelve different ideas, one ranked table.
2. **A domain** (a family flag): the same question put to one family's territory, started from a blank page.
3. **A field** (a topic set): the same plain run, with the chairs of a declared field and not a guess.
4. **A field rebuilt** (the deep setting): rival designs first, three ideas per chair against them, a
   second thinker's audit before the table reaches him.
5. **A field questioned from its purpose** (the Grand Auditorium): the Why diamond first, then he rules on
   the brief, then the deep setting runs against it.

**What the order is not.** It is not a ladder to climb: a small question does not need the Grand one, and
each step costs more calls. The step that matters is between 4 and 5: the deep setting trusts the purpose
it is given, the Grand Auditorium tests it. The topic set is a way of choosing the chairs and can be
combined with the plain Auditorium, the deep setting or the Grand one.

## Which to call

- **You want a spread of ideas on one question, fast:** the plain Auditorium.
- **The question lives inside one domain and you want fresh eyes on it:** that family's flag.
- **You know what the field is for and want it rebuilt, not patched:** the deep setting.
- **The same complaint keeps coming back, or nobody can say what the field is for:** the Grand
  Auditorium. Three runs are on record (`method/double-diamond.md`), each ending in a brief he ruled
  before any How.

## What each costs

**Calls, as counts.** The deep setting and the family flag spend thinker calls, and the session's
standing yes covers five; past five his yes is needed again, per call or once for a named batch. The
token figures for the deep setting are in `protocols/triggers.md`, with a measurement from 2026-11-14
that does not agree with them and is not yet reconciled.

## Limits, stated once

- The deep setting's blind comparison against the plain Auditorium ran once, on 2026-11-03: he preferred
  the deep table, and the structural ideas he would adopt were not counted. A Brass run on
  2026-10-29 is on record for the family flags. The Grand Auditorium has three runs and one reader.
  None of the variants has a counted comparison.
- All chairs are one model, so a variant that convenes more of them is not a more independent check,
  only a wider one. The independent steps are the thinker calls with the pages withheld, and his own
  rulings.
